What we take care of

Every lawn is different. We start by looking at what yours actually needs, then build a plan around it — seasonal or one-time.

  • Fertilization

    Feeding on a schedule timed to how your grass grows, so it greens up in spring and holds through the heat.

  • Weed control

    Pre-emergent treatments to stop weeds before they sprout, plus spot treatment for the ones that sneak through.

  • Fire ant control

    Treating mounds and the yard around them so fire ants stay out of the spaces your family and pets use.

  • Tree & shrub

    Feeding and protecting the trees, shrubs, and beds around your lawn so the whole yard stays healthy.

  • Insect & disease control

    Spotting and treating grubs, chinch bugs, brown patch, and other problems before they spread through the lawn.

  • Aeration

    Pulling cores out of compacted ground so water, air, and nutrients get down to the roots where they count.
